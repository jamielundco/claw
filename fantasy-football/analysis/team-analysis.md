# The Last Blanche — Roster Analysis

## Verdict

The F grade is earned, but it overstates the damage. The real problem isn't
that the players are bad — London and Daniels are legitimately good. It's
that the roster is *shaped* wrong: 5 WRs, 2 QBs, 1 RB, and no kicker or
defense, with 8 of 17 spots empty.

Empty spots are the good news. Most teams come out of a draft full. Eight
open slots plus 3rd waiver priority is real ammunition.

## Priority 2 — Fill K and DEF

Zero cost to fix, pure points left on the table. Stream both — chase
matchup, not name. A defense facing a weak offense outscores a "good"
defense in a bad matchup most weeks.

## Priority 3 — Running back

One RB on a roster needing two starters plus a flex is the structural
hole. Aaron Jones is also the shakiest kind of RB1: 70% rostered but only
7% started, which is the signature of a committee back people hold and
don't trust. He turns 32 this season and Minnesota has invested in the
backfield behind him.

Spend most of the 8 open spots here. In a league this size the waiver
wire won't have starters, so target the two things that actually produce
startable RBs: backups on good offenses one injury from a workload, and
anyone with a pass-catching role (reception volume floors a bad rushing
week).

## Roster inefficiencies to clear

- **Trevor Lawrence** — a second QB in a 1-QB league is a wasted spot.
  Daniels is the better player and the better fantasy build (rushing floor).
  Drop Lawrence for an RB or a defense.
- **Jerry Jeudy** — 13% rostered, 1% started. He is the 5th WR on a team
  that starts 2. Droppable.
- **Jayden Higgins** — if the league has an IR slot, move him there. That
  frees a roster spot for free.

That's potentially three more usable spots on top of the eight.

## Bye week problem

Week 6 takes Aaron Jones and Jordan Addison — your only RB and a starting
WR, both Minnesota. Week 7 takes both quarterbacks and Meyers. Neither is
solvable today, but when you add off waivers, prefer players whose byes
aren't 6 or 7.

## Caveat

Player evaluations here reflect what I know through roughly May 2026. I do
not have 2026 training camp reports, preseason depth charts, or injury news
from the summer. The structural analysis (positional counts, bye
collisions, lineup math) holds regardless. The player opinions should be
checked against current news before you act on them.
