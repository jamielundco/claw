# The last pick: a quarterback — and drop Lawrence for it

## Trevor Lawrence is worth exactly zero to you

He is your backup QB. **He byes in week 7 — the same week as Jayden
Daniels.** A backup whose bye matches your starter's cannot cover the one
week you need covering. He is a roster spot doing nothing at all.

So this isn't really a question of what to spend the pick on. Drop Lawrence,
add a quarterback who actually plays in week 7, and your last pick stays
free for a flier.

## The filter

Week 7 bye teams: **Jacksonville, Washington, Buffalo, LA Chargers.**

Any starting quarterback from the other 28 teams solves it. Sort Yahoo's
available QBs by rostered %, take the highest one not on that list.

## Why this beats a bench stash

Week 7 you currently score **zero at quarterback** — the highest-scoring
position on the roster. A replacement-level starter gets you 15 or so. That
is a certain gain in a week you are otherwise guaranteed to lose.

A WR or TE stash might be worth more, or might be worth nothing. You are
14th; take the certain points.

## Note on the QB pool

26 quarterbacks are already off the board in this league — all 19 rostered
plus Darnold, Cam Ward, Malik Willis, Shough, Daniel Jones, Kyler Murray and
Mendoza from your taken lists. Every QB named as a top streaming target in
preseason coverage is gone.

So don't hold out for a name. Take the best available starter with a
non-week-7 bye and move on.

## Then the actual last pick

With Lawrence's spot covering the QB hole, spend the pick on upside — best
available WR or the Kansas City handcuff. Neither will start for you in week
1, so take the bigger ceiling.
