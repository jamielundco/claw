# Retracting the Kamara pick

## You were right

Alvin Kamara is a bad add and I should have checked before recommending him.

- Age 31, **rushing efficiency at a career low**, on a steady multi-year decline
- New Orleans signed **Travis Etienne to a $48M deal** to be the lead back —
  and Etienne is already rostered in your league by FC Dumpster Fire
- Analysts have Kamara at **RB47**, with "minimal" playing time expected, and
  describe him as "not even a handcuff situation I view as rosterable in
  most formats"

## Why I got it wrong

I pulled him from a single line in a handcuff article calling him the
Saints' "primary pass-catching back" and treated that as a role. The fuller
picture — age, efficiency collapse, and Etienne's contract — says the
opposite. I should have looked at his actual outlook before putting him at
the top of a pick list, especially after flagging Etienne on the New Orleans
roster myself.

## Alternatives, honestly ranked

None of these is exciting. The pool is picked clean and this pick is
low-stakes — your lineup is already complete.

**1. Kansas City's backup running back** (bye 5)
Kenneth Walker III is listed Questionable and is owned by Tijuana Hotdog.
Analysts specifically flag stashing his handcuff as having league-winning
upside, and Kansas City's offense produces. Highest ceiling available.
Check the depth chart for the current name.

**2. Najee Harris** — NY Giants (bye 8)
A real starter-caliber back, healthy again after tearing his Achilles in
week 3 last year, carried 11 times in preseason. He's behind Cam Skattebo
(89% started), so he needs an injury — but he's a genuine NFL lead back if
one comes.

**3. A quarterback for week 7**
Daniels and Lawrence are both on bye that week. You will have zero
quarterbacks. Taking one now with a different bye locks it in — though you
can also just stream in week 6 by cutting Jeudy, which is why this is third
rather than first.

## Recommendation

The Kansas City handcuff. Your running back room is fragile — Jones is a
committee back started in 7% of leagues, Love is a rookie off a high-ankle
sprain, Spears splits Tennessee — so the swing with the highest ceiling is
worth more than another aging body with no path to touches.

Whatever you take, it's a bench player. The pick that mattered was the
defense, and that's done.
