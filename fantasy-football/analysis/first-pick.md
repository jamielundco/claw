15–16 roster spots has 210–225 owned. The 9-round draft left roughly 90
extra players unowned.

That means real starters are sitting in free agency — not just handcuffs
and dart throws. Only 39 running backs are owned in the entire league.

## Who to look for

Sort Yahoo's player list by rostered % and work down the RB list. Target
profile, in priority order:

1. **A starting back on a bad offense.** These fall in drafts because the
   team is bad, but the touch volume is real and weekly. You need someone
   who scores *this* week, in a slot that is currently zero.
2. **Pass-catching role.** Receptions floor a bad rushing week. If your
   league is PPR or half-PPR this moves to #1.
3. **Bye week outside 6 and 7.** Week 6 already takes Aaron Jones and
   Jordan Addison; week 7 takes both QBs and Meyers.

**Avoid pure handcuffs.** A backup with no standalone role scores zero until
an injury that may never come. You have an empty starting slot — you can't
carry a lottery ticket in it. Handcuffs are what the *other* seven open
spots are for.
