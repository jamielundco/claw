that point is settled by your own league data.

**Step 3 — Arizona beats Tennessee on offensive quality.**

Both byes are clean (14 and 9 — you have nobody out either week). The

- Arizona: Trey McBride is started in **98%** of leagues and Marvin
  Harrison Jr. is rostered in 94%. That offense produces.

A starting back on a functioning offense beats a starting back on a dead
one. Arizona also has the better bye for you — week 14, where you currently
lose nobody.

## Why neither Arizona back was drafted

Worth knowing what you're buying. In a 14-team league that drafted 126
players, an established starting RB on a decent offense does not normally
fall. That both Arizona backs went undrafted most likely means the backfield
is a genuine committee, or Conner (who'd be 31) has lost or split the job.

You are buying a murky situation on a good offense. That is still better
than the alternatives, because your RB2 slot is currently a zero — and a
committee back on a scoring offense outproduces a zero every week.

I'm picking Benson over Conner on age and role arc: an undrafted
31-year-old is more likely the one who lost the job than the one who kept
it. That specific call is the weakest link in the chain — my player
knowledge stops around May 2026, so I have no camp reports on how Arizona's
backfield actually shook out. The rostered % on Yahoo reflects what
everyone else has learned since, which is why it overrides me.
