## 1. Defense and kicker — do this first

Two starting slots scoring a guaranteed zero. Worth roughly 15 points a
week, which is more than any bench add will ever give you, and it's
essentially uncontested: one team in your league owns a defense (Briar
Steffenhagen has the Texans) and nobody starts a kicker.

## 2. Tyler Allgeier, if he's still there

You own Love, and Love is about 50/50 for Week 1 with the high-ankle sprain.
Allgeier is trending toward opening as Arizona's primary back and is a flex
play regardless. This is the one contested add on the list — defenses and
kickers will wait, Allgeier won't.

## 3. Clear three dead roster spots

- **Jayden Higgins** — move to IR if your league has the slot. Free spot.

## 4. Week 7 — you will have no quarterback

Daniels (Washington) and Lawrence (Jacksonville) are **both** on bye in week
7. Dropping Lawrence doesn't create the problem, it just makes it visible
earlier. Line up a streaming QB before week 7 and don't get caught.

Also thin: week 11 takes London, Jeudy, and Henry — your WR1 and your TE.

A fourth running back or a sixth receiver. Those sit on your bench. Every
add from here should either fill an empty starting slot or be a handcuff
worth stashing.
